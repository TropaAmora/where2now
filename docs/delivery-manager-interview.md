# Delivery Manager Interview — Discovery Questions

This is a conversation guide, not a form. Ask the questions that feel natural for the flow, skip what doesn't apply, and let the manager talk. The goal is to understand their current pain and whether a route planning tool would add real value — not to pitch anything.

Keep it under 30 minutes. Take notes.

---

## 1. Their current process

- Walk me through what happens the morning before deliveries go out. Who does what?
- How do you decide which driver takes which stops?
- Do you use any software for this, or is it done manually (spreadsheet, paper, WhatsApp)?
- How long does the planning step usually take?

---

## 2. The delivery network

- Roughly how many stops per day, per driver?
- Are the stops mostly fixed (same customers, same addresses) or do they change a lot day to day?
- Do you operate in one city, multiple cities, or a mix of urban and rural?
- Are there stops that are particularly hard to reach or that always cause delays?

---

## 3. Time and reliability

- Do customers expect a delivery window, or just "sometime today"?
- When a delivery is late, what usually caused it?
- Do drivers report back how long each leg actually took, or does that get lost?
- Is there a time of day where things tend to go wrong (traffic, building access, etc.)?

---

## 4. Constraints and rules

- Are there stops that can only be done at certain times? (e.g. a business that closes at noon)
- Any restrictions on what vehicle can go where? (weight limits, narrow streets, etc.)
- Do drivers have fixed shifts, or do you run until the work is done?
- Are there stops that always need to be done first or last?

---

## 5. Pain and cost

- What's the biggest headache in your day-to-day planning right now?
- Have you ever had a situation where a better route would have clearly saved you money or time?
- Do you have a rough sense of how much you spend on fuel per month?
- Has a driver ever had to redo a trip because of a planning mistake?

---

## 6. Data and tools

- Do you have a list of your regular delivery addresses somewhere (spreadsheet, system, etc.)?
- Do you use any GPS or fleet tracking with your drivers?
- Would drivers be open to using an app, or do they prefer not to change their workflow?

---

## 7. Openness to a pilot

- If a tool could take your stop list and give you a suggested route in a few seconds, would that be useful?
- Would you be willing to try something like that for a few weeks and give feedback?
- Is there anything you'd be worried about when adopting a new tool like this?
